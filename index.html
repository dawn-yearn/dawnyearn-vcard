Dating

I like relationships that begin gently. Friends first, usually — just talking, learning how the other person thinks, and seeing whether conversation feels easy and honest. I value openness more than performance: there is no need to keep every guard up, and I would rather have a real conversation than spend weeks trying to look perfectly composed.

That does not mean every conversation has to be profound. Talking about daily life, a favorite singer, a ridiculous story, what we ate today, or where we want to go next can be just as good. Life is not made only of grand meanings.
