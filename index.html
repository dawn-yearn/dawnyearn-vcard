About me

I am an INFP — a little butterfly, if you like that metaphor. I tend to be quiet when I first meet people, but I move through everyday life with a lot of affection, curiosity, and a genuine interest in other people.

I like trying things that are new to me. Lately that has meant exploring AI, learning how to build useful or simply fun things with it, and studying scientific machine learning — especially neural operators such as DeepONet. Outside academics, my interests spread in many directions: books, music, photography, sports, cycling, running, movies, and whatever else happens to catch my attention.

For work, I hope to keep things simple in the best sense of the word: do research that creates real value, make things that people can actually benefit from, and stay close to the human side of whatever I build. Titles matter less to me than becoming someone who cares about people broadly and sincerely.

Connections

I like meeting people, making friends, and getting to know them beyond first impressions. I can be quiet at the beginning, but I genuinely enjoy conversations — sometimes about ideas, values and the way we see the world, and sometimes simply about music, food, a funny story, or how the day went.

I do not think every connection needs to become something in particular. Some people become close friends, some remain brief but memorable encounters, and occasionally someone may become a little more special. I would rather let a connection find its own shape than decide too early what it is supposed to be.

If a connection does become romantic, I still prefer it to grow from friendship rather than from a checklist. I like taking time, being honest with each other, and letting things become serious only when both people want that. If we ever choose exclusivity, I want that choice to be clear and mutual. And I would much rather share ordinary life in the same city than live mostly through a screen.
